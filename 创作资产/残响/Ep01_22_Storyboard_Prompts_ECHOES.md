# 《ECHOES》Episode 01 — 22-Segment Storyboard Prompts (HBO / Apple TV+ Prestige)

> **How to use (3 steps per segment):**
> 1. **① Storyboard image:** Paste the prompt block into GPT mirror site → generates a cinematic production sheet (face strip + 4 frames + action/dialogue/lighting/lens columns).
> 2. **② Feed Doubao:** Send that image + the short Doubao block to Doubao/Jimeng → generates 10s video.
> 3. **③ Assemble:** 22 × 10s clips cut in CapCut by timeline order. Signature transition at **Seg 10→11 (black → data-light)**.
>
> **Trailer switch:** Writing "10-second cinematic trailer" in the prompt = Doubao does fast-cut multi-angle montage. NOT writing it = one continuous shot (more stable, less face-breaking).
> **Trailer ON:** Seg 5, 8, 16, 18, 20, 21 (multi-person / shot-reverse-shot / montage / grand).
> **Trailer OFF:** All other 16 segments (single-person / solitary / hook / lock-off).
>
> **Style lock:** HBO / Apple TV+ prestige sci-fi. Photoreal live-action. ARRI Alexa 65. Anamorphic. Fine film grain. Deep blacks. Naturalistic motivated lighting. Architectural negative space. Uneasy calm. NOT animation, NOT anime, NOT CG-rendered.
> **Duration:** 22 segments × 10s ≈ **3 min 40 sec** (lean cut, no padding).
> **Companion files:** `Character_Scene_Prop_Reference_ECHOES.md` · `第1集_全字段源_残响.md` · `第1集剧本_残响.md`
