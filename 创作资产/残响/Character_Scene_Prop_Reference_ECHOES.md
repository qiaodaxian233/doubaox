## CASTING & VISUAL PRINCIPLES · 选角与视觉原则(先读)

> **【中文】发 YouTube 国际向,这 5 条先定死:**
> **1. 选东亚面孔。** 这是中文故事(沈渡/江临/沈知),东亚班底才真;国际化靠英文配音/字幕,不靠改脸。
> **2. 要"演员脸",不要"偶像脸"。** prestige 的高级感来自有故事感、有皱纹、真实的脸,不是磨皮偶像脸。对 AI 还有个好处:**特征脸更好锁、不容易串脸;通用漂亮脸容易糊、容易换脸。** 出图时多写下面 DO 的词,少写 AVOID 的词。
> **3. 全程实拍质感。** 人、景、物都要像真的,不要 CG 塑料感/动画感。
> **4. 声音走英文。** 脸不变,英文配音或英文字幕冲全球量;数据态那条冷"数据音"英文更有味。
> **5. 封面帧(点击率高):** 段4 沈渡"狂喜冻成恐惧"特写;段20 凶手的手搭在哭泣女儿肩上。

**Prompt word guide (出图措辞):**
- **DO (要写):** characterful, lived-in, weathered, distinctive features, real skin texture with pores, naturalistic, slightly asymmetrical, expressive, gravitas
- **AVOID (别写):** handsome, beautiful, flawless, perfect, idol, model, smooth/poreless skin, symmetrical-pretty, glamorous

## CHARACTERS · 人物

### SHEN DU — Human Form (沈渡 · 人间态)

> **【中文】主角,意识上传科学家,后来变成数字幽灵。**
> **选角:** 东亚中年男(快 50 岁),要"演员脸"不要"偶像脸"——有 gravitas、聪明、疲惫、心里压着事的脸。**别太帅、别太年轻**(会削掉悲剧重量)。皮肤要真实有毛孔,别磨皮。
> **这张脸是全剧招牌**:段4 那个"狂喜→恐惧"的大特写 = 你的 YouTube 封面首选。

**Casting:** East Asian male, late 40s. An **actor-face, NOT an idol-face** — gravitas over good looks. A face that reads "brilliant, exhausted, haunted" before he speaks. Character-actor energy, not leading-man-pretty. Do NOT generate young or conventionally handsome.

**Physical:** 46. Lean, slightly gaunt — a body that forgets to eat. Defined cheekbones, hollows under the eyes. Fine lines at the corners of the eyes — from years at screens, not from smiling. Black hair with premature grey, unkempt. Bloodshot dark-brown eyes. Real, lived-in skin — slightly sallow, visible pores, faint stubble, characterful and slightly asymmetrical. No idealization.

**Wardrobe:** Dark charcoal / slate-grey near-future lab coat over a plain crew-neck. Clean lines, no logos, no flashy tech-wear. Woven fabric with weight and texture. Understated.

**Presence:** Quiet authority. Slow, deliberate. Carries the weight of what he built. When afraid, he goes still. When dying, he doesn't scream — he crawls.

**Lock Notes:** Consistent face across Seg 1–10. No glowing eyes, no facial cracks, no augmentation. He looks normal — that's the point.

**Negative:** anime features, plastic/glossy skin, idol/model face, flawless symmetry, smooth poreless skin, young, conventionally handsome, cyberpunk implants, exaggerated expressions, glamour lighting.

### SHEN DU — Data Form (沈渡 · 数据态)

> **【中文】上传后的数字幽灵。不是人脸,是一团青色明灭、半透明的"数据人形轮廓"。**
> **关键:不要生成任何清晰人脸**;克制不爆闪——它是个勉强撑住形状的幽灵,越不发光越揪心。"睁眼"时只是轮廓中心一个冷光点,不是眼睛。

**Casting:** N/A — **no human face.** Actively AVOID generating any recognizable face.

**Form:** A humanoid silhouette of flowing cyan (teal-blue) light-filaments and data streams. Semi-transparent. Flickering. Unstable. No clear facial features — only the outline of a human shape suggested by flowing data. When he "opens his eyes," it is a single cold point of light at the center of the silhouette — never a face.

**Color:** Cyan / teal-blue primary. Faint magenta accents. Against deep-space black void.

**Behavior:** Flickers, destabilizes when stressed (edges blur, filaments scatter), reconstitutes slowly. Never fully solid. Flares when flooded with data, but never blooms to white.

**Negative:** solid flesh body, any human face, horror/ghost/skeleton, green Matrix rain, cyberpunk neon excess, over-bright bloom, lens flare, Tron suit.

### JIANG LIN (江临)

> **【中文】徒弟,失手害死师父的凶手——但是个灰色人物,不是坏人。**
> **选角:东亚男,35 上下,关键是"可信的脸"——要让观众一开始愿意相信他、甚至喜欢他,后面才够痛。** 英气、可靠、像个好徒弟。**绝对不要反派脸**(别加疤、别加阴森光)。"看起来是好人"才是这个角色的恐怖。

**Casting:** East Asian male, mid 30s. **Crucially: a trustworthy, likeable face — NOT a villain face.** The audience must want to believe him, even like him, early on. Hard-handsome and intense is fine (the polished protégé), but real and characterful, not a soft idol. The horror is that he looks like a good man.

**Physical:** 34. Slightly broader than Shen Du. Sharp jaw, strong brow. Light stubble. Dark, neat hair. Dark intense eyes with the fevered gleam of a true believer. Lived-in, real skin.

**Wardrobe:** Dark navy / black high-collar research jacket — tailored, severe. Press conference (Seg 18): a dark formal suit, composed but slightly hollow.

**Presence:** Restless conviction. Speaks with his hands. His stillness after the push (Seg 6–7) is terrifying because he was never still. His gentleness toward Shen Zhi (Seg 20) is genuine — and that's what's unbearable.

**Lock Notes:** Consistent across lab (4–9), press conference (18), memorial (20). Multi-image face lock critical.

**Negative:** villain coding (scars, sinister under-lighting, dark-circle caricature), overt menace, anime features, plastic skin, soft-idol face, flawless symmetry.

## SCENES / ENVIRONMENTS · 场景

> **【中文·实景原则】** 每个场景都要像真实地点或真实搭景、真实打光,不要 CG 渲染感、不要游戏关卡感、不要贴图。

### DU CONSCIOUSNESS LABORATORY — 2099 (渡氏意识实验室)

> **【中文】用于段1–10(第一幕全部)。** 一个空旷、冷白、过度干净的高级实验室:中央环形神经接驳台 + 空扫描椅,三面环形全息屏,人被巨大空间衬得很小。墙边有机柜(含角落那台关键备用服务器)。冷白无影灯 + 屏幕青光,唯一的颜色是青色屏光;销毁时渗红光,上传时红翻青。气质:临床、压抑、"为一个想法盖的教堂"。

**Architecture:** A single vast room. Oppressively high ceiling. Circular neural interface rig at center with an empty scanning chair. Three curved holographic display walls. White, grey, steel, glass — architectural minimalism that makes one person feel very small. Personal terminal in one corner. Server racks along one wall (incl. the corner backup server). Metal floor with subtle reflection.

**Lighting:** Cold surgical white overhead + cyan screen glow. No warm light. Motivated, naturalistic. Deep corner shadows. Never flatters.

**Color:** High-key whites, steel greys, deep blacks. Only color = cyan screen glow. Deletion = red warning light; upload flips red to cyan.

**Negative:** cyberpunk neon, rain, grime, Star Trek sleek, bunker, CG-render set, matte painting, game level.

### DATA REALM — Server Interior (数据界 · 服务器内部)

> **【中文】用于段11–16。** 不是宏大数字神殿——是一台小备用服务器内部:逼仄、昏暗、像数据做的棺材,通道窄如囚笼。唯一光源是青色数据光,其余全是深空黑。倒计时=红字;格式化=吞噬一切的冷白浪潮。逃出去后空间豁然开阔(棺材→海洋)。

**Space:** Abstract digital space inside a single backup server. NOT a grand cathedral — cramped, dark, claustrophobic, a coffin of data. Narrow data-channel corridors. Walls are data-streams, not solid.

**Lighting:** ONLY light = cyan data-glow. Everything else deep-space black. Countdown adds red text; formatting wave is blinding cold-white erasure.

**Color:** Deep black, cyan/teal, faint magenta, red countdown, white wave.

**Negative:** green Matrix rain, Tron grid floors, cyberpunk neon city, horror/flesh walls, grand cathedral, bloom excess.

### 2099 CITY — NIGHT (2099 城市夜景)

> **【中文】用于段17–18、21–22。** 近未来大都市:干净、有序、被算法管得井井有条,几何高楼、车流光带、到处是摄像头/屏幕(藏在建筑里,反而更瘆人)。冷蓝市政灯,无暖色。气质:完美却无魂。段21 全城摄像头/屏幕同时亮起冷光时,城市没变,但你忽然意识到——它在看着你。

**Architecture:** Near-future megalopolis. Clean, ordered, algorithm-managed. Uniform geometric towers. No chaos/grime/neon-overload. Glass, concrete, soft white/blue municipal light. Cameras & sensors everywhere, integrated into architecture.

**Lighting:** Cool blue municipal LED. Vehicle light-trails. Cold white/blue public screens.

**Color:** Steel blue, concrete grey, cold white. No warm tones.

**Negative:** Blade Runner neon, rain, flying cars, decay, grime, utopian-shiny, dystopian-grimy, CG-render cityscape.

### MEMORIAL HALL (追思会现场)

> **【中文】用于段19–20。** 极简肃穆的追思会厅,沈渡遗像 + 白花,空旷到衬出女孩一个人的孤独。整体冷调,**唯一例外**:一束克制的暖光落在沈知身上(全片唯一的暖)。江临走近时,他的影子开始吞掉那点暖。

**Architecture:** Large minimalist memorial hall. Clean lines, muted materials (stone, wood, brushed metal). Shen Du's portrait on a simple stand. White flowers. Too big for one girl kneeling alone — the emptiness is the point.

**Lighting:** Cold institutional overall. EXCEPTION: a single subtle warm light on Shen Zhi — the ONLY warm light in the episode. Jiang Lin's shadow eats the warmth.

**Color:** Cold institutional grey/white. Warm accent on Shen Zhi = muted gold, human, not Hollywood-warm.

**Negative:** ornate, religious iconography, dark/gothic, CG-render set.

## KEY PROPS · 关键道具

> **【中文·实物原则】** 每件道具都要像真造出来的、真实打光的设备,不要"发光的科幻道具"感、不要 CG 资产感。

- **Neural Interface Rig (环形神经接驳台)** — 【中文】实验室中央的环形接驳装置 + 一把人体工学扫描椅,拉丝钢 + 哑黑关节,头顶有可下放的扫描臂。冷白光下像个祭坛。
  *Ring-shaped apparatus, brushed steel + matte-black joints, ergonomic scanning chair, overhead articulated scanning arms. Looks like an altar under surgical white light. Real industrial-design feel, not a movie prop.*

- **Consciousness Map Hologram (意识神经图谱)** — 【中文】中央大屏上缓慢旋转的 3D 人脑神经网络图,青色光,角标"COMPLETENESS 100.0%"。克制、像真实的数据可视化,不是花哨 HUD。
  *Slowly rotating 3D neural network, cyan light, "COMPLETENESS 100.0%" in minimal sans-serif. Restrained, real data-viz, not a flashy sci-fi HUD.*

- **Wangchuan Protocol Interface (忘川协议界面)** — 【中文】主控台软件,两态:销毁=红字"SOURCE DATA DESTRUCTION · 0%"+进度条+红光;上传=青字"CONSCIOUSNESS UPLOAD · INITIATED · 11"+倒计时,红翻青。极简真实终端,不堆 HUD。
  *Two states — Deletion (red, "SOURCE DATA DESTRUCTION · 0%") / Upload (cyan, "CONSCIOUSNESS UPLOAD · INITIATED · 11" countdown). Minimal real terminal UI.*

- **Scanning Probe (扫描探头)** — 【中文】贴太阳穴的小型精密探头,细线连着接驳台,哑黑医用级。沈渡把它贴上自己太阳穴那刻不夸张——一个小小的临床装置,却是生死/数字之门。
  *Small temple-mount probe on a thin cable, matte black, medical-grade. Understated — a small clinical thing that is the doorway between life and digital existence.*

- **Backup Server Rack (备用服务器)** — 【中文】实验室角落一台不起眼的机柜,灰金属、线缆、一排指示灯,其中一颗蓝白灯稳定地一闪一闪。全集最重要的物件,却长得毫不起眼。关灯后,它是画面里唯一的光——一次心跳。
  *Unremarkable corner rack, grey metal, cable bundles, one blue-white LED blinking steadily. The most important object in the episode, looking like nothing. After lights-out, the only light in frame — a heartbeat.*

- **Memorial Portrait (沈渡遗像)** — 【中文】追思会上沈渡的正式遗像,端庄体面——和实验室里那个血丝满眼、疲惫的他完全不像。遗像与真人的落差,本身就是悲剧的一部分。
  *Formal, composed portrait — the public face of a great scientist, unlike the exhausted bloodshot man from the lab. The gap is part of the tragedy.*

- **Surveillance Camera (监控摄像头)** — 【中文】普通市政摄像头,小、嵌在建筑里、容易忽略。沈渡"接上"它时,镜头里有一点极淡的青色反光。到段21,全城几百上千个同时亮起这点光——"烛龙"千眼初开。
  *Standard municipal camera, small, integrated, easy to miss. A faint cyan reflection when Shen Du connects. By Seg 21, hundreds flicker citywide — the birth of Zhulong's (烛龙) thousand eyes.*
